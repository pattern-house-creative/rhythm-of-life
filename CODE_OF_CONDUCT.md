# Code of Conduct

Rhythm of Life is software for a household's private life. The people who build it should be as
considerate with each other as the software tries to be with its users.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Assume the other person means well.
- Talk about the work, not the person. Disagree with reasons.
- Welcome newcomers. A question that seems basic to you was new to everyone once.
- Respect privacy. Never post anyone's personal or health data, real tokens, or details of someone
  else's install. Use fictional examples (the repository's own are `alex` and `sam`).
- Accept that maintainers decide what goes in, and that "no" or "not now" is an answer.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or unwelcome personal attention of any kind.
- Discrimination or demeaning remarks about who someone is.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Deliberately disrupting discussions or reviews.

## Where this applies

Everywhere the project lives: issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and any other place where
someone is acting as part of the project.

## If something goes wrong

Tell the maintainers privately. Use the repository's private reporting form (the **Security** tab,
**Report a vulnerability**), which only the maintainers can read, and say that it is a conduct report.
You can also use the hosting site's own "Report content" link on any comment.

Maintainers will read every report, keep the reporter's identity private, and act in proportion: a
quiet word, a warning, removing content, or removing someone from the project's spaces.
