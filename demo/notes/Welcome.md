# Welcome

This folder is a fictional sample for the `notes` adapter. Point `RHYTHM_NOTES_DIR` at it to see how a
Markdown folder becomes stars: every note is a star, links become solid lines, and folders and tags
become projects.

- The week starts with the [[Weekly review]].
- Alex keeps a [[Bike commute|commute log]]; Sam counts glasses of water in [[Water intake]].
- The house has its own folder: [[Home/Boiler service]] and [[Paint colours]].
- The [[Book club]] picks its [next book](Reading/Next%20book.md) in turns.

Nothing here is about a real person. #sample
